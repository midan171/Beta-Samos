Safety & FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

Safety & Driver Regulations

🪪

Valid Driver's License Required

Drivers must hold a valid physical car driver's license (EU Category B or International Driving Permit).

⛑️

Mandatory Helmets

Safety is our #1 priority. DOT/ECE helmets are provided and mandatory during all ATV riding segments.

👥

Age Limits

Drivers must be 18+ years old. Passengers must be at least 8 years old.

☀️

Weather & Cancellation Guarantee

Free cancellation up to 24h before tour. 100% full refund or reschedule in case of severe weather.
