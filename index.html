Traveler Preparation

Before You Ride: Important Info

Everything you need to know for a smooth, safe, and exciting excursion on Samos.

1

Driver's License & Age

All participants must be between 21 and 74 years old. Drivers must hold a valid physical Category B (car) license.

2

Proper Footwear

Closed-toe sneakers or hiking shoes are mandatory for driving and hiking. No flip-flops or open sandals allowed.

3

Small Group Excursions

Excursions are limited to a maximum of 8 participants for optimal safety, comfort, and personalized guidance.

4

Weather Guarantee

In case of extreme weather, reschedule for free or receive a 100% full refund.
