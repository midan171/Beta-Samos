# Beta Samos ATV and Hiking Tour - Company Profile

## Overview
**Beta Samos ATV and Hiking Tour** (Legal Entity: **BETA SAMOS MONOPROSOPI IKE**) is a premiere outdoor adventure and tour guide service based on Samos Island, Greece. We specialize in guided off-road ATV rides combined with scenic hiking excursions, revealing the island's hidden viewpoints, mountain tracks, coastal paths, and cultural heritage.

- **Brand Tone**: Adventurous, welcoming, safety-conscious, authentic, local, and professional.
