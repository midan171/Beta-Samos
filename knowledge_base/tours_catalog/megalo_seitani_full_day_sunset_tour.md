## Highlights
- **Potami Waterfalls & Nature Hike**: Walk scenic river paths and swim in natural fresh water pools.
- **Pythagoras Cave & Mount Kerkis**: Explore the historic cave and drive thrilling high-altitude mountain trails on Mount Kerkis.
- **5 Traditional Samian Villages**: Off-road safari passing Marathokampos, Paleochori, and remote authentic villages.
- **Authentic Tavern Lunch in Drakei**: Savor traditional Greek cuisine and hospitality in a remote cliffside village.
- **Megalo Seitani Secluded Beach**: Hike to Samos' most famous untouched beach with crystal turquoise waters for swimming and relaxing.
- **Golden Hour Coastal Sunset Drive**: End the grand day expedition driving alongside the glowing Aegean sunset.

## Full Itinerary
1. **Departure & Potami Waterfalls (10:00 - 11:30)**: Meet at Karlovasi base for briefing and gear fitting. Drive to the lush Potami waterfalls for nature hiking and refreshing swimming.
2. **Pythagoras Cave Hike & Mount Kerkis Exploration (11:30 - 13:30)**: Ride ATVs up to the historic Cave of Pythagoras on Mount Kerkis for hiking and guided exploration, followed by dramatic off-road mountain ridge trails with Aegean panoramas.
3. **5 Villages Safari & Drakei Tavern Lunch (13:30 - 15:30)**: Traverse scenic countryside through Marathokampos, Paleochori, and 5 traditional villages. Arrive in Drakei for a feast at a local tavern.
4. **Megalo Seitani Guided Hike & Swimming (15:30 - 18:30)**: Park ATVs and embark on the scenic coastal nature hike to the untouched beach of Megalo Seitani. Enjoy leisure swimming, sunbathing, and exploration.
5. **Seaside Coastal Excursion & Sunset Return (18:30 - 20:00)**: Ride back along coastal seaside tracks as the golden sun sets into the Aegean Sea.
