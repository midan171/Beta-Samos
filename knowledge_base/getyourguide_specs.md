# GetYourGuide (GYG) Listing Standards & Requirements

## Overview
This document specifies GetYourGuide's content standards, character limits, formatting rules, and optimization guidelines for Beta Samos listings.

## Listing Field Requirements & Constraints

### 1. Activity Title
- **Format**: `[Location]: [Activity / Main Experience]` or `[Location]: [Main Feature] [Activity Type]`
- **Examples**:
  - `Samos: Guided ATV Off-Road Tour & Nature Hike with Snacks`
  - `Samos: Off-Road Quad Bike Adventure & Scenic Hike`
- **Character Limit**: 30 - 80 characters.
- **Rule**: Avoid buzzwords like "Best", "Unforgettable", "Top Rated" in titles (GYG guidelines penalize clickbait titles).

### 2. Short Description (Catchline)
- **Character Limit**: 100 - 200 characters.
- **Goal**: Highlight the key value proposition in 1 to 2 crisp sentences.
- **Example**: *"Explore Samos on a guided ATV off-road tour combined with a scenic nature walk. Drive through pine forests, discover hidden mountain viewpoints, and enjoy local snacks."*

### 3. Bullet Point Highlights
- **Count**: 3 to 5 concise bullet points.
- **Character Limit**: Up to 120 characters per bullet point.
- **Action Verbs**: Start each bullet point with strong action verbs (Drive, Discover, Hike, Taste, Enjoy).

### 4. Full Description
- **Structure**:
  - **Paragraph 1**: Introduction to the experience, starting location, and overall vibe.
  - **Paragraph 2**: The ATV driving segment, route highlights, and scenery.
  - **Paragraph 3**: The guided hiking segment, nature exploration, and local snack break.
  - **Paragraph 4**: Wrap up, returning to base, and key takeaways.
- **Formatting**: Plain text with clear paragraph breaks. No HTML tags or excessive ALL CAPS.

### 5. Mandatory Inclusions & Exclusions List
- Clear bulleted list.
- Explicitly detail equipment provided (helmets, ATVs, fuel), guide, snacks, water.

### 6. Important Information / Before You Go
- Drivers must bring valid original driver's license.
- Closed-toe shoes mandatory.
- Minimum age requirements (18 for driver, 8 for passenger).
- Not suitable for pregnant women or people with back problems.
